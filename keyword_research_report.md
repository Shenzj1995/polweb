## Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive keyword research analysis for building an AI video and image generation SaaS platform similar to pollo.ai. The AI video generation market has seen explosive search volume growth since late 2022, driven by tools like Runway, Sora, Kling AI, and Pika. The market is projected to continue growing rapidly through 2026-2027.

Key findings:
- **"AI video generator"** is the highest-volume head term (est. 300K-500K monthly global searches)
- **Long-tail keywords** present the best opportunity for a new platform to rank
- **Comparison keywords** (e.g., "Kling AI vs Runway") have high commercial intent
- **Model-specific keywords** (Seedance 2.0, Veo 3, Sora 2) are emerging rapidly
- **Pollo.ai uses a programmatic SEO strategy** targeting every model name + feature combination
- **Use-case keywords** (e.g., "AI video for social media") are underserved and high-intent

## Keyword Difficulty Assessment

### Difficulty Scale
- **Very High (85-98):** Dominated by established brands (Google, OpenAI, Adobe). Extremely difficult for new sites.
- **High (70-84):** Competitive but achievable with strong content and link building over 6-12 months.
- **Medium (50-69):** Achievable for new sites with good content within 3-6 months.
- **Low-Medium (35-49):** Achievable within 1-3 months with quality content.
- **Low (20-34):** Quick wins. Achievable within weeks with basic optimization.

### Recommended Keyword Targeting by Site Age

**Months 1-3 (New Site):**
Focus on Low and Low-Medium difficulty keywords:
- Viral effect keywords (AI hug generator, Ghibli AI generator)
- Emerging model keywords (Seedance, Vidu AI, Wan AI)
- Niche use-case keywords
- Specific tool keywords (AI object remover, anime upscaler)

**Months 3-6:**
Expand to Medium difficulty:
- Comparison keywords (Kling AI vs Runway)
- Model + feature combinations
- Free-tier keywords with modifiers
- Use-case + platform keywords

**Months 6-12:**
Target High difficulty:
- Head terms with modifiers (best AI video generator 2025)
- Major model keywords (Kling AI, Runway AI alternative)
- Feature head terms (text to video AI, image to video AI)

**12+ Months:**
Challenge the highest volume terms:
- Pure head terms (AI video generator, AI image generator)
- Major brand alternatives (Midjourney alternative, Runway alternative)

## SEO Strategy Recommendations

### 1. Programmatic SEO (Highest Priority)
Create landing pages for every combination of:
- Model + Feature: "Seedance 2.0 text to video", "Kling AI image to video"
- Feature + Use Case: "text to video for marketing", "image to video for social media"
- Model + Use Case: "Runway for YouTube videos", "Kling AI for ads"

Estimated pages: 200-500+ unique landing pages

### 2. Comparison / Alternative Pages
Create dedicated pages for:
- Every "[Competitor] alternative" keyword
- Every "[Model A] vs [Model B]" comparison
- "Best AI video generator for [use case]" roundups

Estimated pages: 50-100 comparison pages

### 3. Viral Effect / Trend Pages
Quickly create pages for trending effects:
- Monitor TikTok/Reddit for new viral AI effects
- Create dedicated generators/tutorials for each
- These can drive massive short-term traffic spikes

Estimated pages: 30-50 trend pages (constantly updated)

### 4. Blog / Content Cluster
Build topic clusters around:
- "AI video generation" (pillar) with 20-30 supporting articles
- "AI image generation" (pillar) with 20-30 supporting articles
- "How to" guides for every feature
- Tutorial content for every supported model

Estimated pages: 100-200 blog posts

### 5. Free Tool / Freemium Pages
- Create free versions of tools to capture high-volume "free" keywords
- Gate advanced features behind sign-up
- Use free tools as lead magnets

### 6. Technical SEO
- Ensure fast page load times (Core Web Vitals)
- Implement structured data (FAQ, HowTo, SoftwareApplication schemas)
- Create XML sitemaps for all programmatic pages
- Optimize for mobile (majority of searches are mobile)
